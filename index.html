The “flaky network” that wasn’t

Dropped calls down ~10%, CSAT up ~15%.

Churn you can engineer down

Directed the cross-functional rollout of traffic prioritization, congestion throttling, and satellite load balancing — network capabilities sold internally as experience improvements, measured like it.

Independent applied work

My own projects and portfolio learning — personal tooling, personal budget, and the discipline I’d bring on day one.

Tested like it matters — for free

My own evaluation practice runs on a 40-case test set: held-out prompts across accuracy, grounding, refusal, privacy, and escalation, with risk scored as likelihood × impact — including residual risk after controls. Weekly reviews fine-tune the pipeline against the rubric, and the generator and the evaluator are never the same LLM: a different model judges every run. The whole thing runs on a Telegram bot + Replit with free open-source Groq models — keyword mapping handles the high-confidence cases, an LLM round-robin picks up the low-confidence ones when a model exhausts its free limits or fails. Cost was a design constraint from day one.

The evaluation discipline most teams skip.
